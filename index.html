About Me

I am a visual storyteller and photographer dedicated to capturing authentic moments across street, documentary, and maritime environments. With a foundation rooted in photojournalism and editorial storytelling, my work revolves around observing the subtle interplay of natural light, human resilience, and shifting atmospheres.

My approach is deeply rooted in minimalism—stripping away distraction to let the core narrative speak for itself. Whether framing a coastal squall, documenting daily coastal livelihoods, or isolating a quiet moment on the street, I focus on composition, timing, and atmospheric light.
