# 📷 Visual Portfolio & Gallery

> **🌐 View Live Gallery:** [Click here to view the live site](https://your-live-link-here.com)

A curated collection of visual stories, photojournalism, and coastal landscapes. This gallery documents the raw beauty of maritime environments, changing atmospheric light, and the daily rhythms of coastal communities.

---

## 📸 Featured Collections

### 1. Coastal Livelihoods & The Sea
* **Focus:** Documentary & Photojournalism
* **Themes:** Island resilience, traditional fishing craft (*bangka*), and shared community labor along the shore.
* **Mood:** Raw, narrative-driven, authentic.

### 2. Shifting Horizons & Maritime Light
* **Focus:** Fine Art Landscape & Atmospheric Photography
* **Themes:** Dramatic cloud formations, golden hour reflections, and high-contrast coastal horizons.
* **Mood:** Atmospheric, expansive, contemplative.
